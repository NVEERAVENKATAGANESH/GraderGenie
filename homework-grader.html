About Rubrics

Rubrics ensure consistency across your class — every student is assessed on the same criteria.

The default rubric covers four criteria: Thesis & Argument (30 pts), Evidence & Sources (25 pts), Analysis & Depth (25 pts), and Writing & Clarity (20 pts).

Click score buttons on each criterion, then hit Apply to Grade to transfer the percentage to the Grade Submission form.

Tip: Complete the rubric before writing feedback so you have a grade context to work with.
